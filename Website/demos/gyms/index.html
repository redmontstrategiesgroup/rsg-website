System breakdown

Automation does the chasing. Your team does the judging.

What enters the system

  • Trial request forms
  • Instagram & Facebook DMs
  • Missed calls & texts
  • Walk-in sign-ups
  • Class attendance data
  • Member milestone events

What the system does

  • Qualifies every inquiry by goal, experience, and schedule
  • Recommends the right class or program
  • Books trials with waivers and instructions
  • Runs reminder and no-show recovery sequences
  • Sends post-trial membership offers
  • Triggers referral requests at member milestones
  • Tracks attendance, conversion, and follow-up status

What your team still controls

  • Coaching, programming, and the classes themselves
  • Membership pricing and offers
  • Personal calls to hot or hesitant leads
  • Sensitive member conversations
  • Which sequences run and how they sound
  • Reviewing conversion performance
