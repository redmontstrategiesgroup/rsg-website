System breakdown

Automation does the chasing. Your team does the judging.

What enters the system

  • Instagram & Facebook DMs
  • Missed and after-hours phone calls
  • Website consultation forms
  • Text messages
  • Existing patient records
  • Lapsed-lead and no-show lists

What the system does

  • Captures every inquiry into one record
  • Asks qualifying questions and collects contact details
  • Sends booking links and consultation reminders
  • Runs reactivation campaigns on lapsed leads
  • Creates front-desk tasks with context
  • Flags no-show risk and negative feedback for staff
  • Tracks response time, conversion, and show rates

What your team still controls

  • Approving campaign messages before they send
  • Clinical questions and treatment recommendations
  • Sensitive patient conversations
  • Performing consultations and treatments
  • Pricing, offers, and membership decisions
  • Adjusting any workflow, reminder, or sequence
